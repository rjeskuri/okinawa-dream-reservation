<h2 id="license-classes">License classes</h2>

The license classes by engine size:

- Up to 50 cc, or a 125 cc bike limited to 4.0 kW (a rule since April 1, 2025): moped or car license
- Up to 125 cc: small-motorcycle license
- Up to 400 cc: standard motorcycle license
- Above 400 cc: large motorcycle license

<h2 id="international-permits">International permits</h2>

Visitors need one of the following:

- An international driving permit under the 1949 Geneva Convention, with category A for motorcycles and category B for cars.
- An official Japanese translation, such as JAF's, for licenses from Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Monaco or Taiwan.

Either is valid for 1 year from arrival.

Permits under the 1968 Vienna Convention are not accepted.

<h2 id="payment-cards">Cards accepted online</h2>

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club and JCB online.

UnionPay is not accepted online.
